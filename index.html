About

gaoren:// is an experimental web project that serves as both a personal digital space and an evolving archive. The archive consists of several independent modules; each section covers its own subject and format without adhering to a common structure or visual system. The project is intentionally open-ended and is expected to grow over time.

I am a student of Information Systems and Programming, currently studying web development independently alongside my formal education. My strongest area is frontend development, although my interests extend into software, Linux, programming languages, and technology in general. Most of the things I build are personal projects, made to solve my own problems or to explore an idea that interests me.

I am particularly interested in making things from beginning to end, from the way they look and feel to the way they work. Rather than following a fixed design system or established conventions, I usually make decisions according to what I find practical and comfortable to use. This approach is personal rather than professional, and I do not consider myself an expert in any of these fields.

Outside of programming, I have a general interest in technology and material culture, particularly older and obsolete technologies. CRT televisions/monitors, VHS tapes, books, manga, comics, games, and various forms of physical media are among the things I collect or explore. I am also interested in Japanese culture, particularly anime, manga, and older games, and hope to visit Japan someday.

Most of my interests overlap in one way or another, but they do not necessarily belong together. Some become projects, some become written notes, and some remain simple interests. I prefer to keep them separate rather than forcing everything into a single theme.

gaoren:// is maintained as an independent personal project. It is not intended to represent a particular profession or identity, but simply to provide a place for things I make, study, collect, and think about. The site will change as these interests change, and its structure is allowed to change with it.
